About me

In My Own Words

Who I am, where I come from, and what I have been looking for through the lens for since 1999.

I was born in Budapest in 1979 and I have been photographing since 1999 — more than half my life. Today I live and work in Vienna, with one foot always in Budapest. If you ask what my work is about, I would say: people at their truest. Women rebuilding themselves after illness. Fathers learning a new kind of strength. The memory of 1956. Dancers between discipline and freedom. A lone figure crossing a New York street at dusk.

My road was anything but straight. I studied engineering, then founded the photo studio of the Hungarian Defence Forces — that is where I learned to work when the stakes are real. New York gave me four wild years: exhibitions, books, studies at the New York Institute of Photography. Since 2018 I have been an OM SYSTEM Ambassador, and from 2020 I co-founded and curated the digital Rege Gallery in Tihany, where we played with The Frame, NFTs and AI long before it was fashionable to ask what images are becoming.

There is one thing I never put in a CV, and it is probably the most important. Around 2013 I went to Greece to rest. First day, I took my rollerblades down to the sea and, for once, left the protective gear behind. There was gravel before a bend, I was going fast, and I went into a wall. My elbow was shattered, my face was torn open by my own sunglasses, and I could not see out of one eye.

What happened next still surprises me: a huge calm came over me and the army training simply switched on. Shirt off, arm tied up, find where the blood is coming from, apply pressure, flag down a motorbike. In the hospital a doctor who had already worked twenty-four hours decided to stay with me. We talked all night in a language neither of us spoke well.

Lying on that bed, not knowing whether I would keep my sight, I understood something. I had done a great many things — photographer, marketer, event organiser, community builder — and I had not truly found my place in any of them. It was position-collecting, not a life. So I made a deal with myself: if I survive this, I do photography. Everything you see on this site comes from that night.

These days much of my energy goes into community: portrait fundraisers for the Vienna Hungarian School, and the free Be Smart Kids Club I started for Hungarian-speaking children. Photography gave me everything — this is how I give some of it back.

Publications

Books

Three interdisciplinary books, each made together with writers, doctors and the people in the pictures. Open one for its full story and gallery.

2017

Awakening: The New Beginning! — Book

A photographic book about twelve women reclaiming life after gynaecological cancer. Their portraits, scars and own words make recovery visible without turning it into spectacle.

ISBN 978-963-12-8663-2

Book page →

2017

Snippets

A collaborative book in which poetry, performance, photography and moving image meet. What began as an experiment among friends became a printed record of how one work can be experienced through several senses.

ISBN 9786150000534

Book page →

2018

The World of Woman

Nine stages of a woman’s life are brought together through portraits, literary reflection and personal testimony. The book follows change without prescribing a single definition of womanhood.

ISBN 978-615-00-1829-4

Exhibitions

Nineteen completed exhibitions and one project in development, from Budapest and New York to Vienna and Munich. Each has its own page with the story behind it and its images.

2014

Real Women — 30+1 Portraits of Natural Beauty

Stefánia Palace, Budapest

This was my first real statement, and I still stand by every word of it. Thirty-one women, no retouching, no tricks, no digital surgery.

2014

Those Old Flirty Times — Pin-Up Rehabilitated

Millenáris Velodrom, Budapest

A contemporary reading of pin-up made at the Millenáris Velodrom. The series looks past costume and nostalgia to the self-aware confidence carried by the women in front of the camera.

2014

Hungarian Women in New York

Hungarian House, New York

New York changed the way I see. Not the way I shoot — the way I see.

2015

50 Shades of Woman

Stefánia Palace, Budapest

Fifty portraits explore how identity shifts between expectation, private experience and self-image. The exhibition treats womanhood as a spectrum rather than a fixed role.

2016

I Became a Father

Bálna, Budapest

A personal portrait series about men crossing into fatherhood. Made as my own life changed, it records uncertainty, responsibility and the quiet ways identity is rearranged by a child’s arrival.

2016

Milestones ’56

Hungarian House, New York

In New York I met people who carried 1956 in their pockets. Literally: photographs, a key, a christening spoon — the one object they grabbed on the way out.

2017

Snippets

3 September 2017 · Stefánia Palace, Budapest · later toured in Hungary

A travelling exhibition where poetry, performance and photography shared the same space. Each venue reshaped the work, turning a collaborative experiment into an evolving public encounter.

2017–2018

Awakening — The New Beginning

2017–2018 · Budapest · Noszvaj · Veszprém

Portraits and testimony by women rebuilding their relationship with their bodies after gynaecological cancer. Shown across several Hungarian locations, the exhibition made survival and recovery publicly visible.

2018

The World of Woman

7 September 2018 · Stefánia Palace, Budapest

Nine transitions every woman passes through — puberty, motherhood, menopause, ageing, all the stations the fashion world pretends do not exist.

2018

Ballerina Project New York — Strut Your Stuff

Long Island, New York

New York dancers leave the stage for rooftops, crossings and streets. The series places disciplined movement inside an unpredictable city and watches what happens when precision meets chance.

2018

Contemporary Artists of Pest County

25 January 2018 · City Hall, Budapest

A group show at Budapest City Hall where my New York photographs represented Gödöllő, of all places. I rather enjoyed the irony of that — a small Hungarian town speaking through pictures of Manhattan.

2021

The Frame — 20 Years

Rege Gallery, Tihany

A twentieth-anniversary exhibition presented entirely on digital displays at Rege Gallery. The changing sequence made the method of presentation part of the retrospective rather than a neutral frame around it.

2021

You Can Be Too…

Rege Gallery, Tihany

Created with the Hungarian National Ballet Institute, this project follows young dancers through rehearsal, repetition and physical effort. It shows the daily work behind the possibility of becoming a ballet artist.

2022

The Men’s Dream — The Genesis of Beauty

Club Skinos, New York

A fine-art nude project shown alongside a charitable NFT auction in New York. It examines how authorship, ownership and authenticity change when a photographic work enters digital circulation.

2023

The False Faces of Reality / The Lies of the Internet

Rege Gallery, Tihany

AI-generated portraits were exhibited using the visual conventions of documentary photography. The project tested how readily viewers accept a convincing image as evidence of a person who never existed.

2024

Touch Vienna & Touch Munich

14 February 2024 · Vienna · 15 May 2024 · Munich · Everness, Siófok

Photographed in Vienna, Munich and at Everness, the series documents the attentive human dimension of tantra massage. Its focus is trust, care and the respectful encounter between bodies.

2024

I Am the Woman

Bewusst Sein Im Fokus, Vienna

No single definition of womanhood survived this project, thank goodness. It became a collective mosaic — many women, many contradictions, no conclusion offered.

2025

Femme Fatale

2 March 2025 · CITYgalleryVIENNA, Vienna

An archetype everyone thinks they know. Together with the women I worked with in Vienna we read it again from the beginning — and found something much more interesting than the cliché.

2025

Different Image / Different

Veszprém

Zsófia Szele writes poems about chronic illness and caregiving, and I answered them with photographs. Quiet work, made in Veszprém, about the kind of endurance nobody applauds.

2027

EUFÓRIA — The Anatomy of Presence

Exhibition in development · November 2027
