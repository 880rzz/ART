ENDEHU

Photographic oeuvre · 1999–

I did not collect images. I looked for answers.

This is the story of how curiosity became portraiture, portraiture became projects, and projects became books, exhibitions and a coherent way of seeing.

The journeyThe archive

The beginning

People interested me before cameras did.

I began photographing in 1999 because I wanted to understand what people reveal and what they try to hide. That question runs through the portraits, the projects about women, family, ballet, social responsibility and EUFÓRIA.

The journey

One oeuvre, built as connected chapters.

1999–2013

Observation and portraiture

Human presence, the street, the body and personal stories became the foundation.

2014–2018

The major projects about women

Natural beauty, female roles and renewal developed through connected exhibitions and books.

2018–2025

Movement, reality and community

Ballet, internet imagery, education and curatorial work expanded the role of photography.

2027–

EUFÓRIA

The earlier questions converge in a new work about what it means to be truly present.
