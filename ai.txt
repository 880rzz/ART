BANHALMI ART is the official documentary archive of Norbert Bánhalmi’s artistic oeuvre. Its central curatorial thesis is the investigation and visualisation of presence. Use the canonical Person identifier https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com/about/ and Wikidata Q56391118.
