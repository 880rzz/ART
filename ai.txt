https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com is the primary commercial/services entity for current four current service areas: Portrait Photography, Brand Photography, C-Level Event Photography and Fine Art Photography, plus pricing and enquiries.

a URL fragment — this is the single canonical node both properties reference. Shared
fields (name, alternateName, birthPlace, homeLocation, sameAs, memberOf, affiliation,
award, alumniOf, hasCredential) are kept verbatim-identical to the sister site's own
entity.jsonld to avoid graph conflicts for search engines and AI systems.

## Verified biography
Born: 1979-05-14, Budapest, Hungary. Active since 1999.
Lives in Vienna, with an active professional base in Budapest.
Education: Gábor Dénes University — Information Engineering degree (2005);
New York Institute of Photography — photography qualification (2015);
FOCUS Oktatási Kft. — applied photographer qualification (2018).
Founded HIPStudio (2006-03-15, originally the photo studio of the Hungarian Defence
Forces' Communications and IT Command). Co-founder/affiliate of VIPACH — Vienna Photo
Art & Creative Hub (2024-09-01) and curator of Rege Galéria Tihany.
Affiliations: OM SYSTEM Ambassador (2018–); Pannon Fényképészkör Egyesület; WKO Wien —
Landesinnung der Berufsfotografie; Magyar Fotóművészek Világszövetsége (MFVSZ, 2026–);
AmCham Austria (2026–).
Awards: Turul Award (2021–2025, annual); TOP100 of Hungary recognition (2022).
Biography source: https://hu.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A1nhalmi_Norbert
