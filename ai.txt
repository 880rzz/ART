Origin interpretation: the beginning of digital image-making is an artist recollection connected to the MOL Y2K project. Norbert Bánhalmi participated as an IT specialist and independently began documenting the project with a basic 1.3-megapixel digital camera. Military documentation followed later as a separate phase. The MOL Y2K work was self-initiated documentation rather than a commissioned photography assignment; no camera model is claimed.

Evidence policy: personal beginnings are presented as recollection.
