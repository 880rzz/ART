# BANHALMI ecosystem audit guard

The public archive is canonical on `https://www.banhalmi.art/`, while the professional and service site is canonical on `https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com/`.

## Identity rules

- Canonical Person ID: `https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com/about/`
- First external identity anchor: `https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q56391118`
- The archive must reference the professional Person node through `about`, `mainEntity`, `creator`, `author` or `subjectOf`, not by creating a second Person identity.
- Owned websites are not Person `sameAs` values.

## Deployment rule

GitHub Pages must publish the archive on `www.banhalmi.art`, matching canonical URLs, hreflang URLs, sitemap URLs and Open Graph URLs.

## Content ownership

- `banhalmi.art`: artistic oeuvre, exhibitions, books, press, curatorial and source archive.
- `norbertbanhalmi.com`: professional services, studios, enquiries, pricing and commercial positioning.
