huende

Bánhalmi Norbert

1979, BudapestBécs · Budapest1999 —

Archival record — the body text is preserved in its original Hungarian.

As a child I wanted to be an astronaut, to explore the unknown. In the end I set out not toward the stars but into the depths of the human soul — though the desire to discover stayed the same.

He originally worked as an IT professional, until in 1999 his uncle handed him his first inspiration for photography. Fittingly, his creative awakening took place in the strictest of environments — during professional military service, where he fell irrevocably in love with digital photography.

This path led him to found, on 15 March 2006, the professional photo studio of the Hungarian Defence Forces' Communications and IT Command — HIPStudio for short. After leaving service he kept the brand name, turning it into a professional photo/video company that marked its 20th anniversary in 2026.

The Beginnings: From Coca-Cola Projects to His Own Path

After leaving the military, civilian work drew him in. To this day he is proud that more than 100 young couples trusted him with their wedding photography, while his creative energy also found an outlet in campaigns for world brands such as Coca-Cola. But the real breakthrough — and his own voice — he found in New York in 2014.

New York and the "Paper of Qualification"

The pulse of the city and its artistic milieu taught him not just to look but to truly see. One of the key milestones of his professional development was earning his New York photography qualification — not merely a certificate, but the cornerstone of his international outlook. This perspective gave rise to the exhibition Milestones '56, processing the stories of Hungarians living in emigration.

More Than Photography: The "Soul Project"

For him, photography is also a form of social responsibility. He believes art can help — whether the subject is self-acceptance or charitable initiatives such as "The Great Rescue Action." This "seeing beneath the surface" approach also brought major professional recognition: in 2018 he was named brand ambassador of Olympus | OM System, as curator of the Rege Gallery at the Tihany Benedictine Abbey he conceived a digital gallery together with Samsung, and he served as official photographer for the Dutch national football team's EURO campaign.

In-depth interviews and press echo

He believes in honesty. In recent years he has had the opportunity to speak with several leading media outlets — not only about successes, but also about difficulties.

The Fulfilment of a Professional Path

The past quarter-century of creative work reached another defining milestone in 2026: after 26 years, the World Federation of Hungarian Photographers (MFVSZ) accepted him as a member. He now lives a life divided between Budapest and Vienna, passing on what he has learned through the VIPACH photo club. He is proud that, from 2026, as a member of the American Chamber of Commerce Austria (AmCham Austria) he also strengthens international business and artistic ties between Budapest, Vienna and New York.

Professional recognitions: Turul Award (2021–2025, annual), TOP100 of Hungary recognition (2022). His foundational degree is a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Engineering from Gábor Dénes University (2005).
