Comprehensive Curatorial Report & Oeuvre Analysis

2014 — 20254 periods

The analysis below is a curatorial text drawn from the exhibitions archive page. It distinguishes four creative periods: deconstructing female identity (2014–2015), depths — intimacy and trauma (2016–2018), digital horizons (2021–2023), and return to the body (2024–2025).

1. Deconstructing Female Identity and Body Image (2014–2015)

The goal was to dismantle the myth of retouched perfection and visually rehabilitate natural beauty: The Real Women, Those Old Coquettish Times, Hungarian Women in New York, Fifty Shades of Woman.

2. Depths: Intimacy, History and Trauma (2016–2018)

A turn from the surface toward depth-psychological processes and historical memory: I Became a Father!, Milestones '56, Awakening — A New Beginning, Szösszenetek, The World of Woman, The Ballerina Project.

3. Digital Horizons and Generative Art (2021–2023)

A dialogue between human creation and algorithm, a critique of digital reality: The Frame, You Too Can Be…, The Men's Dream (NFT), The False Faces of Reality.

4. Return to the Body: Sacredness and Touch (2024–2025)

As a counterpoint to digital alienation, a turn toward flesh-and-blood reality and the healing power of touch: Touch Vienna/Munich, I the Woman, Femme Fatale, Different/IMAGE/Different.
