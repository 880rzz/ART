The Artist Behind the Business

2001 — 2021Záridő Fotós Magazin

Career interview — 20th professional anniversary

In 2021 I celebrated my 20th professional anniversary. Counting everything it is already the 22nd, but this is the milestone from which I count the years. On this occasion Bandi Roszberger, editor-in-chief of Záridő Photo Magazine, wanted to know where I started, who I was, and who I am now. This interview was a great honour for me.

Please receive it warmly. This is the story of my photographic life so far.
