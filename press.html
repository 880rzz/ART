Press archive · 2014–2026

Presence in the public record

Interviews, reports, features and moving-image records—not as a publicity dump, but as chapters in how a lifelong investigation of presence became publicly visible.

The same question keeps returning: when is a person, a memory or a community truly present? These records show how the public interpretation of the work moved from female identity and historical memory through recovery, curation and community-building to leadership presence.

Curatorial proposition

The investigation of presence

One consistent impulse runs through the entire oeuvre: to recognise and make visible the moment in which someone is no longer merely performing a role, but is genuinely present. The subjects changed—women, fathers, survivors, historical witnesses, artists, leaders and communities—but the question remained.
