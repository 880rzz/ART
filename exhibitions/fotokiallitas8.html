Exhibition · 2018

Contemporary Artists of Pest County

25 January 2018 · City Hall, Budapest

In my own words

A group show in Budapest City Hall where my New York photographs represented Gödöllő, of all places. I rather enjoyed the irony of that — a small Hungarian town speaking through pictures of Manhattan.

But it made sense in the end. Local identity and the wide world are not opposites; they are the two ends of the same rope. Most of us spend our lives holding on somewhere in the middle.
