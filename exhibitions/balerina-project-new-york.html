Exhibition · 2018

Ballerina Project New York — Strut Your Stuff

Long Island, New York

In my own words

Ballerinas fascinated me not on stage but off it. On rooftops, on crosswalks, in the two seconds before a taxi horn. Everything they do on stage is controlled to the millimetre; I wanted the millisecond where control lets go.

New York gave me the backdrop — concrete, steam, that hard morning light between buildings. The dancers gave everything else. There is a particular kind of courage in doing a perfect arabesque on a filthy street corner while the city ignores you completely.
