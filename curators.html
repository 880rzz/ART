The Anatomy of Presence

Curatorial dossier

The investigation of presence

This dossier reads the oeuvre through its central proposition: presence is not a pose or an appearance, but the moment in which a person, memory or community becomes perceptible without being reduced to a role. The humanist lens is the method; the investigation of presence is the work.

For curators and institutions

The archive brings together projects, books, exhibitions, public reception and supporting documentation so that each chapter can be read within the development of the complete oeuvre.
