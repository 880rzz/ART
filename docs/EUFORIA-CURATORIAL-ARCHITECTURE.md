# EUFÓRIA — Curatorial Architecture Contract

Status: project-level source of truth
Scope: BANHALMI ART EUFÓRIA, 2027 exhibition build, related Blog and Professional projections

## Curatorial premise

EUFÓRIA — The Anatomy of Presence is conceived as a twenty-work portrait project: twenty people encountered in twenty distinct moments. The project is not organized around fame, profession, political affiliation, or a single social category. Each chapter asks what becomes visible when a person, a specific moment and the photographer's presence coincide.

The first two chapters are Péter Magyar and Azahriah. Eighteen further chapters remain open until the corresponding works and moments exist and can be documented.

## Mandatory chapter architecture

Every person/work chapter follows the same six-part sequence:

1. PERSON — Who is this person?
   A concise, human introduction. Include only biography needed to understand the work: field, cultural/social role, and relevant context. Do not turn the page into a biography or celebrity profile.

2. MOMENT — Why this moment?
   Exact date/place/context when known. Explain why the situation matters to the work. Separate verified historical facts from contemporary interpretation.

3. PHOTOGRAPH — What happened in the frame?
   Describe the encounter and the visible photographic decisions: gaze, gesture, distance, light, framing, environment, timing. Never invent camera settings or private intent.

4. READING — What can the image mean?
   Curatorial and photographic interpretation. Clearly label interpretation as interpretation. Avoid political endorsement, psychological diagnosis, heroization and inflated claims.

5. AFTERLIFE / PROVENANCE — What happened to the image?
   Creator, date, location, licence where relevant, archive identifiers, exhibitions, publications, editorial/social reuse and external attribution. Reuse proves circulation only; it does not prove endorsement, commission, partnership or client status.

6. EUFÓRIA CONNECTION — Why is this work part of the twenty?
   State the specific form of presence this chapter contributes. Do not force later works into the categories established by earlier works.

## Person rule

The human introduction should normally answer in 60–120 words:
- Who are they?
- What field or public/cultural context makes them relevant to this particular moment?
- What does a curator need to know before looking at the photograph?

It must not become:
- a complete CV;
- fan copy;
- political persuasion;
- celebrity biography;
- SEO biography;
- unsupported personality analysis.

## Twenty-work rule

The website must always distinguish:
- completed/documented works;
- works in development;
- the planned twenty-work project as a whole.

Never visually imply that two existing works are the whole exhibition. Never invent the remaining eighteen people or moments before they are selected and photographed.

## Cross-site ownership

BANHALMI ART = canonical project and curatorial authority.
Photography History / Fotótörténelem / Fotogeschichte = editorial story, photographic observation and cultural context; it links to ART for the complete project.
BANHALMI-C Professional = concise authorship/professional evidence; it links to ART for artistic/project meaning.

No site may reproduce the full canonical project description from another site.

## 2027 rule

Until venue, dates and production details are confirmed, describe 2027 as the planned exhibition horizon / exhibition in development. Do not publish unverified venue, opening date, institutional partnership or 'largest exhibition' as an externally established fact. Internally, the project may be designed to support the intended major 2027 exhibition.

## Curator usability test

A curator arriving without prior BANHALMI knowledge should be able to answer within one page:
- What is EUFÓRIA?
- Why twenty people and twenty moments?
- Who is the person in this chapter?
- Why did this particular moment matter?
- What did Bánhalmi photograph rather than merely document?
- Which statements are facts and which are interpretation?
- What evidence establishes authorship and circulation?
- How does this work change or expand the meaning of the complete project?
