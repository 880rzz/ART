# BANHALMI ART — public editorial and confidentiality contract

The public site speaks in the first person where Norbert Bánhalmi is the author.
The tone is precise, personal, humane and restrained. Every artistic claim must
stay distinct from independently verifiable dates, exhibitions, credits and
publisher evidence. Do not invent quotes, visitor reactions, outcomes or
confidential behind-the-scenes details. EN and DE-AT preserve the meaning and
natural tone of the Hungarian original rather than translating idioms literally.

Private communications may be reviewed in a connected, permissioned environment
to understand writing style. **No email, message, attachment, sender/recipient,
conversation URL, direct quotation or identifiable client detail may be copied
into this repository or embedded in generated pages, source comments, test
fixtures, metadata, PR descriptions or deployment artifacts.** Do not commit
personal writing samples, even in redacted form. Author new public-facing prose
from already public, independently justified facts.

Publication changes require:
- explicit public-source / author-verified support for factual statements;
- source-safe, non-duplicated person/project and artwork references;
- no release of confidential, classified, financial, health, identification,
  credentials or non-public third-party information;
- review of image rights, consent and sensitive art-project narratives;
- EN/HU/DE-AT editorial parity without losing authorial distinctions;
- the repository correspondence scanner and the editorial voice regression gate;
- PR review, required CI, GitHub Pages exact-live verification.

Automated scans are a baseline, **not a guarantee**. Review the Git diff and
commit history before production publication; a private value can evade a regex.
Never weaken or skip a publication gate to make a release green.
