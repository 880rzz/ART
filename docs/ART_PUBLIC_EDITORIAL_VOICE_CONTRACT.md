# BANHALMI ART — public editorial voice and privacy contract

Scope: all canonical EN, HU and de-AT public ART pages. This contract defines only publishable writing standards; it contains no correspondence samples, private source text or client information.

## Author voice
- Write creator-authored passages from Norbert Bánhalmi's personal, concrete viewpoint: what I photographed, why I worked on the subject and what mattered in the actual encounter.
- Keep the person and the photograph first. Prefer short to medium sentences and observable detail to grand claims, superlatives, speculation or invented inner states.
- Preserve clear distinctions between original art and commissioned photography; ART is a cultural archive, not a professional-services clone.
- In book pages, the photographic contribution can be first-person; bibliographic authorship, clinical information, exhibition dates, publication data and source credits remain factual and independently verifiable.
- EN, HU and Austrian German reflect the same facts and intent, but are idiomatic in their respective languages; do not mechanically translate idioms.
- Exhibition history, institutional records, press quotations, metadata, rights notices, consent boundaries, official original titles, canonical URLs and schema are not treated as creative copy.

## Privacy and release
- Do not add or reproduce private email text, direct quotes from correspondence, email IDs, recipients, client/customer lists, internal pricing, unpublished requests, private photographs, personal secrets, access credentials, tokens, business negotiations or correspondence-derived facts.
- Historical correspondence, if privately consulted for style, is never a published source and must not be committed as raw text, extracts, embeddings, statistics, fixtures, examples, screenshots, or provenance metadata.
- Only independently public, approved claims may appear in page content; mark uncertain chronology as approximate. Keep author, contributor and publisher roles distinct.
- All changes go through branch, PR, CI (including the correspondence publication gate), GitHub Pages release and exact-live review. No source-to-live claims from a PR alone.
