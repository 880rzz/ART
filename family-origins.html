Origins · family background

Family origins

On my mother’s side, the family story reaches back to the Cseuz line.

The Cseuz line

The surviving family trees describe the Cseuz line as having Transylvanian roots. In older records, the name also appears in the form Csausz. What interests me here is not a title or inherited rank, but the way members of the family repeatedly left something tangible behind through learning, work and building.

Historical sources connected with the Csausz–Cseuz name preserve the stories of physicians, a university professor and royal councillor, a military doctor, and specialists in industry and mill engineering. Our own family tree does not prove direct descent from every known person, so I do not want to claim more than the documents can support. The recurring attitude is clear enough: acquire knowledge, make something useful, and put that knowledge to work.

The Ferenczy line

Through Etelka Cseuz, the story continues into the Ferenczy family. Here the presence of making and art becomes more immediate. József Ferenczy created woven works built from geometric forms. Róza Margit Ferenczy and her husband, Kálmán Győry, taught and painted in Sopron.

I do not think of them as an artistic dynasty. I think of them as people for whom drawing, painting, working with materials and passing on knowledge were simply part of everyday life.

The immediate family environment

That attitude continued in later generations. My mother, Erzsébet Rózsa Győry, is an interior designer, painter, visual artist and natural therapist. My father, Gábor Bánhalmi, designed individual furniture as a wood-industry engineer.

László Győry, my mother’s brother, worked as a photographer in Sopron. He did not teach me photography, and there was no master–student relationship between us. Even so, photography was already present in the family world long before I picked up a camera myself.

I did not inherit a finished artistic programme. I inherited the sense that planning, making, understanding and passing something on can be a natural part of life.

Source background: the Csausz–Cseuz and Cseuz–Ferenczy family trees, public historical and biographical sources connected with the Csausz–Cseuz name, and Norbert Bánhalmi’s family account. The wider history of the name does not automatically prove direct descent from every historical person mentioned.
